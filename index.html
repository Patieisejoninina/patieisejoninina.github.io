🧭 What Is crystaldiskmark-desktop?

crystaldiskmark-desktop is a small Windows program that acts as a helper companion for CrystalDiskMark, the well-known disk speed testing tool. CrystalDiskMark measures how fast your hard drive or SSD can read and write data. That is genuinely useful information — but over time, the test results, settings files, and temporary export caches can end up scattered in folders you do not remember the name of.

This desktop helper solves that small but annoying problem. It gives you one clear place to look at the data paths CrystalDiskMark uses, review and reset your configuration files, clear out export caches that are taking up space, and jump straight to the folder where your exported results live.

You do not need to be a programmer, a system administrator, or a computer expert to use it. If you can click a button and open a folder, you can use this application. Everything is designed around plain language and short, obvious steps.
